# 微创心脏搭桥术中切除Marshall韧带预防术后新发房颤的多中心随机三盲研究

**原文：**The Effect of Ligament of Marshall Excision During Minimally Invasive Cardiac Surgery-Coronary Artery Bypass Grafting (MICS-CABG) on the Prevention of Postoperative New-Onset Atrial Fibrillation: A Multicenter, Prospective, Randomized, Triple-Blinded Study
**来源：**ClinicalTrials.gov
**链接：**[DOI](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07755072)

## 核心发现
该研究评估在微创心脏搭桥术中切除Marshall韧带对预防术后房颤的效果。术后房颤发生率为20%-40%，微创手术中为4.8%-18.4%。

## 与研究方向关联
不直接相关，主要涉及心脏手术技术，与营养或菌群无关。
